Name the four VTP roles and what each does with VLANs and VTP advertisements.	<b>Server</b>: creates, modifies and deletes VLANs for the domain.<br><b>Client</b>: takes its VLANs from advertisements and can't configure VLANs locally.<br><b>Transparent</b>: <b>forwards</b> advertisements but doesn't apply them. Its VLANs are configured locally.<br><b>Off</b>: doesn't take part in VTP and doesn't forward advertisements. Its VLANs are configured locally.<br><br>(There is no "proxy" role.)	ENCOR ch05 VTP
VTP versions: which is the default, which VLAN range do the versions carry, and how many servers can a domain have?	Version <b>1</b> is the default.<br>v1 and v2 carry only VLANs <b>1-1005</b>. v3 carries the full <b>1-4094</b>.<br><br>A domain can have <b>multiple servers</b>. In v3, only the <b>primary</b> server (set with the exec command <code>vtp primary</code>) can add or remove VLANs.	ENCOR ch05 VTP
What are the three VTP advertisement types, and which one actually carries the VLAN changes?	<b>Summary</b>: every 300 s or on a change. It carries the version, domain, <b>revision number</b> and timestamp, but <b>not the VLANs</b>.<br><b>Subset</b>: sent after a change. It carries the VLAN details.<br><b>Client request</b>: a switch sees a higher revision in a summary and asks for the subset.	ENCOR ch05 VTP dikta
List the VTP configuration steps in order. Which step resets the revision number?	1. <code>vtp version {1|2|3}</code><br>2. <code>vtp domain &lt;name&gt;</code>: changing the domain <b>resets the revision to 0</b><br>3. <code>vtp mode {server|client|transparent|off}</code><br>4. <code>vtp password &lt;pw&gt;</code> (optional, recommended)<br>5. v3 only: <code>vtp primary</code> (exec mode, not config)<br><br>Verify with <code>show vtp status</code>.	ENCOR ch05 VTP config
You move a switch from a lab into the production VTP domain without resetting its revision number. Why can this be a disaster?	If its revision is <b>higher</b>, the domain treats it as the newest database and every switch takes its VLAN list.<br>If it's missing VLANs, those VLANs get <b>deleted across the whole domain</b>.<br><br>Fix: reset the revision to 0 before connecting it (e.g. change the domain name and change it back).	ENCOR ch05 VTP troubleshooting
The VTP server is at revision 1 and you create VLANs 10, 20 and 30. What revision does it show, and how does a client behind a transparent switch get the VLANs?	Revision <b>4</b>: it goes up by 1 for each change.<br><br>The transparent switch forwards the advertisements without applying them, so the client still gets revision 4 and VLANs 10, 20 and 30, with no CLI work on the client.	ENCOR ch05 VTP
What does DTP do, how often does it advertise, and what must match between the two switches?	DTP (Dynamic Trunking Protocol) sends packets to <b>negotiate</b> whether a link becomes a trunk.<br>It advertises every <b>30 seconds</b>.<br>The <b>VTP domain must match</b> on both switches.
